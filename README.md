# **👣** Stride

**Progress that moves with you**

Not a habit tracker. Not a to-do list. A goal-execution system for people who already know what they want, they just need the structure, the streak, and the social mirror to keep going.

## Market gaps

The research identified four consistent failure points across every major accountability app. These are your competitive advantages â€” build what they broke.

### What every competitor gets wrong

#### Streak data loss, the unforgivable sin

Coach.me users report 1,000+ day streaks randomly erased. `"For an accountability app built on streaks, losing streak data is unforgivable."` Habi review. Your streaks must be bulletproof, backed up, and recoverable.

#### Gamification wears off after weeks

Habitica's RPG mechanic is fun at first, then the novelty fades. The game becomes routine and stops driving behaviour. You need progression that deepens rather than repeats.

#### Social accountability is either absent or expensive

Apps either have no social layer at all, or charge $25-$249$/month for human coaching. The middle ground, lightweight, free peer accountability, is completely empty.

#### Setup requires too much willpower to start

`"If the setup process itself requires willpower, the app has already failed."` Most apps overwhelm new users with configuration before they've done a single check-in. You should be accountable within 60 seconds of opening it.

#### No goal-type awareness

Every app treats `"meditate daily"` and `"launch a product in 90 days"` the same way. They're not. One is a habit, one is a project milestone. The tracking, the streaks, and the check-in format should be different.

### How Stride fixes each one

#### Streak data is sacred, cloud-synced, exportable, always yours

Every streak backed up in real time. If a streak breaks due to a bug, it's restorable. Users can export their full history at any time. This alone differentiates you.

#### Progression that evolves, not just points

Streaks unlock new features, not just badges. A 30-day streak unlocks Accountability Circles. A 90-day streak unlocks your public profile card. The app reveals itself as you earn it.

#### Free peer accountability, the missing middle

Pair with one other user as an accountability partner. Share streaks. Get notified when they check in. Celebrate their wins. No coach needed, just a real person watching.

#### 60-second onboarding, first check-in before you leave the page

Name your goal. Set your daily check-in time. Check in once. Done. Everything else is optional and reveals gradually. No configuration walls.

#### Two goal types, daily habits and milestone projects

Habit goals track streaks daily. Project goals track milestones, each one ticked moves a progress bar toward a deadline. Different UI, different check-in, same motivational engine.

## Feature Set

Designed in three tiers, `what launches (V1), what grows (V2), and what scales (V3)`. Every feature earns its place by solving a real gap or creating a feeling competitors don't.

### V1, Launch features (build these first)

#### 1. Streak system with freeze protection

**V1 Free**

Daily check-in builds a streak. Miss a day and it breaks, unless you use a Streak Freeze (2 free per month, more via paid). Streaks are cloud-backed and never lost to bugs. You can share your streak as a card, like Duolingo, to any platform. Streak milestones (7, 30, 60, 90 days) trigger a moment, animation, a message, a shareable graphic.

**Gap fixed: streak data loss + no shareable social proof**

#### 2. Two goal types, Habit & Milestone

**V1 Free**

Habit goals: daily check-in, streak counter, pass/fail. Simple. Milestone goals: a project with a deadline, broken into steps. Each step ticked moves a progress bar. End date creates urgency. Both types live on the same dashboard â€” different visual treatment.

**Gap fixed: no goal-type awareness in any competitor**

#### 3. Daily mission, tonight's one sentence

**V1 Free**

Every evening, Stride prompts: `"What is your mission for tomorrow?"` One sentence. Saved. Shown to you first thing when you open the app the next morning. This is the single highest-leverage feature for goal-activated people â€” it removes the drift.

**Built directly from your personal operating insight**

#### Weekly review, 5 questions, 5 minutes

**V1 Free**

Every Sunday, a simple prompt appears: 

1. What did you do? 

2. What did you skip? 

3. What got in the way? 

4. What did you learn? 

5. What's your mission next week? 

Answers saved as a private log. Over time this becomes a journal of your growth, searchable, exportable.

**Gap fixed: no app currently does structured reflection*

#### 60-second onboarding

**V1 Free**

Name - Goal type - Goal name - Check-in time - First check-in. Done. User is accountable before they leave the landing page. No profile setup. No preferences wall. Everything else is optional and appears as they use the app.

**Gap fixed: setup requiring willpower before first use**

### V2, Growth features (after launch)

#### 1. Accountability Partner, 1-to-1 pairing

**V2 Free**

Pair with one other user. See each other's streaks and daily missions. Get a nudge notification when your partner checks in or misses. Send a `"push"`, one tap that sends `"Your partner is watching"` to the other person. No chat, no complexity. Just presence. This is the missing middle between solo apps and expensive coaching.

**Gap fixed: social accountability only available at premium price**

#### 2. Stride Circles, small group accountability

**V2: Paid WOW feature**

Groups of 3-5 people working toward their own individual goals but checking in together. Daily group streak, if everyone checks in, the group streak grows. If one person misses, no one loses their personal streak, but the group streak breaks. Creates team pressure without team dependency. Unlocked at 30-day personal streak OR paid tier.

**The Duolingo leaderboard feeling, without the toxic competition**

#### Shareable streak card

**V2: Free, Viral feature**

At streak milestones, 7, 30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days, Stride generates a beautiful shareable card. `"Day 30. Goal: GICSP certified. Still going."` Designed to share on LinkedIn, Twitter, WhatsApp. The card has the Stride logo, every share is free marketing. Users will share because it makes them look disciplined, not because you asked them to.

**This is how the app grows itself zero ad spend required**

#### Streak Freeze, grace day system

**V2: 2 free/month, More via paid**

Life happens. 2 free streak freezes per month, use one and your streak is protected for that day. You still have to check in the next day. Additional freezes available via paid tier. This reduces the `"I missed one day so I quit"` dropout pattern that kills engagement in every competitor.

**Duolingo's most effective retention mechanic, adapted for goals**

### V3 Scale features (when you have users)

#### Public profile your growth record

**V3 Paid**

A public URL `stride.app/yourname` showing your active goals, longest streaks, milestone completions, and weekly review summaries (optional). This is your verifiable growth record. Professionals share it on LinkedIn. Students share it in applications. It becomes a proof-of-discipline document.

**Turns Stride from a personal tool into a public credential**

#### Progress analytics, patterns over time

**V3 Paid**

1. Which days do you check in most? 

2. Which goals do you skip? 

3. What time of day are you most consistent? 

Patterns shown visually, heat map, consistency graph, goal completion rate. Strides is the only app that shows you your own accountability patterns, not just your streaks.

**Gap fixed: no competitor offers self-insight analytics**

## Monetisation

Free gets you users. Paid gets you revenue. The freemium model works here because the free tier is genuinely useful, and the paid tier adds things people actually want, not features you've artificially locked.

### Free tier, always free

Unlimited goals (habit + milestone). Daily check-in and streak. Tonight's mission. Weekly review. 2 streak freezes per month. Shareable streak card at milestones. This is genuinely complete not crippled. Users trust you because the free tier respects them.

## Stride Pro 3-5/month or $25-35/year

Accountability partner pairing. Stride Circles (groups of 3-5). Unlimited streak freezes. Advanced analytics and patterns. Public profile URL. Priority streak recovery if a bug causes data loss. At $3/month this is an impulse buy, lower friction than Spotify.

### Stride Teams, $20-50/month (future)

For companies, bootcamps, cohort programmes. A facilitator creates a group, members join, everyone's goals and check-ins are visible to the group. Weekly review summaries go to the facilitator. Universities, accelerators, and online courses are the target. This is the B2B play that scales revenue.
